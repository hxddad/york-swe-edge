## ENG 4000 - Engineering Project

This will be your capstone project you'll be working on throughout the fall and winter semester in a team, oh yeah this is also a year course (6 credits). You will be working with an advisor, a professor from Lassonde, plus you may work closely with companies regarding the project including AMD, SmartTO, k2i, PLC Group, MDA Space, etc.

Now for the projects, you get to either choose a project from a list or propose your own. Me and my team went with a fully software full-stack project for a company, since it's easy to collaborate on and we didn't have to buy anything. Depending on the project, you may have to buy physical materials and parts (with reimbursements) for mechanical or electrical based projects. 

All of these projects are large-scale which cannot be completed overnight. So depending on the project, you will have to plan in advance on how to complete and organize the project. After all the effort, it'll be worth completing as you know have an impressive project to talk about on your resume and interview.

## EECS 4312 / 4313 / 4314

These are software engineering and architecture courses involving completing a project with a team. These are more project based classes, so they are not too difficult to pass.

This is essentially a fullstack project course, where you get to build a e-commerce platform! You get to learn and try some tools used in those projects in the industry. Although, I honestly think it should be a 3rd year course instead of having to wait until 4th year to take it.
